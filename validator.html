Multinode inventory validator for Kolla-Ansible

Reads an INI inventory and checks it: the presence and membership of the control, network, compute, storage and monitoring groups, duplicate hosts, empty groups, contradictory variables and syntax errors. Optionally takes a globals.yml and adds the rules that need both files at once. Everything is analysed in your browser — the file is never uploaded or stored. Scope and limits are described in SCOPE.md.
